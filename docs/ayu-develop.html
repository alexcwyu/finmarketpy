Design decisions

Decision Rationale Tradeoff Evidence
Represent strategy performance as aligned return matrices. Vectorized signal accounting supports basket and risk comparisons. It approximates trading costs without an order acknowledgement/fill lifecycle. Backtest.calculate_trading_PnL (L121)

Strengths and limits

Strengths

  • Risk targeting and portfolio construction are visible in the signal/PnL pipeline. Backtest.calculate_trading_PnL (L121) / PortfolioWeightConstruction.optimize_portfolio_weights (L2522) / RiskEngine.calculate_position_clip_adjustment (L2880)

Limitations

  • RiskEngine absolute exposure branch references portfolio_abs_exposure established in the net exposure branch. Static review does not establish that max_abs_exposure alone is safe. RiskEngine.calculate_position_clip_adjustment (L2880)
